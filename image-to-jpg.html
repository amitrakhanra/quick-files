FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About Image to JPG

Convert PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP pictures to the widely supported JPG format. Many forms and websites only accept JPG, and JPG files are usually smaller than PNG.

Choose a quality level (High, Good or Small file) and convert one image or many together. Pictures with transparent areas get a white background, because JPG cannot store transparency. Animated GIFs are saved as a single still picture.

How to convert images to JPG

  1. Choose your PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP images.
  2. Pick the quality: High, Good or Small file.
  3. Click Convert to JPG.
  4. Download the JPG, or a ZIP if you converted several.

Tips and limits

  • Choose Small file when you need a lighter image for uploading. Choose High for photos you want to keep.
  • Converting a JPG to JPG again only loses quality, so there is no need to run those through this tool.
  • To make a PDF from the converted pictures, use JPG to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparent PNGs?

The transparent area becomes white, because the JPG format has no transparency.

Can I convert an animated GIF?

You get a single still JPG picture from it, not an animation.

Can I set an exact file size?

Not here. Use Compress Image to a Specific Size to reach a size such as 50 KB.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
