FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About Add Watermark

Stamp a line of text such as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT or your name across every page of a PDF. A watermark makes it clear what a document is, and discourages people from passing it off as final or as their own.

The text is placed diagonally across the middle of each page in grey, sized to fit the page. You choose the wording and how strong it looks: Light, Medium or Strong. The pages stay as normal PDF pages, so the original text remains selectable.

How to add a watermark to a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Type the watermark text, for example DRAFT.
  3. Pick the opacity: Light, Medium or Strong.
  4. Click Add watermark. The new PDF downloads.

Tips and limits

  • Keep the text short, one or two words work best.
  • The watermark can only use standard Latin letters, numbers and common punctuation. Other alphabets are not supported yet.
  • A watermark is a visual mark, not a security feature. It is not designed to stop someone from copying a document.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put a watermark on only some pages?

Not at the moment. The watermark is added to every page.

Can I use a logo or an image?

No. This tool adds text watermarks only.

Can I remove a watermark afterwards?

Keep your original file. The watermark is drawn onto the new copy, and QuickFile does not offer a way to remove it.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
