FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About Compress PDF to a Specific Size

Reach the exact file-size limit that a form, portal or email asks for. Type the size you need, in KB or MB, and QuickFile tries progressively smaller settings until the PDF fits.

Pages are saved as images to make the file small, so the text in the result is not selectable. If your PDF is already under the target nothing is downloaded, and if the limit is too tight you are told the smallest size that could be reached.

How to compress a PDF to a specific size

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Type your target size, for example 200, and choose KB or MB.
  3. Click Compress.
  4. Download the result and check its size.

Tips and limits

  • The smallest target accepted is 10 KB.
  • Very small targets on long PDFs may not be reachable. If so, try a larger target or split the PDF first.
  • Check that the pages are still readable before you upload, especially if they contain small print.

Frequently asked questions

What if the target cannot be reached?

QuickFile gives you the smallest result it could make and says that the target was not reached, so you can decide what to do.

Will the text stay selectable?

No. This tool rebuilds each page as an image, so selectable text is lost.

Are there ready-made sizes?

Yes. See Compress PDF to 100 KB and Compress PDF to 200 KB.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
