FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About Compress Image to a Specific Size

Get a photo or signature under the size limit of an online form. Enter your limit in KB or MB and QuickFile finds the best quality that fits, shrinking the picture's dimensions only if it has to.

The result is a JPG. It first tries high quality, then lowers the quality step by step, and finally reduces the pixel size if quality alone is not enough. If even that cannot reach your limit, you are told so and can try a larger one.

How to compress an image to a specific size

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Type the target size, for example 50, and choose KB or MB.
  3. Click Compress.
  4. Download your JPG and check that its size is under the limit.

Tips and limits

  • The smallest target accepted is 2 KB. Very small targets make pictures blurry.
  • Signatures and passport-style photos shrink well. Detailed photos need larger targets.
  • Check the width and height your form asks for as well. Use Resize Image if you need exact pixel dimensions.

Frequently asked questions

Will it always reach my target?

Usually, but not always. A very small limit on a large detailed picture may be impossible, and QuickFile tells you when that happens.

What format is the output?

JPG, regardless of the format of the original.

Are there presets?

Yes. See Compress Image to 20 KB, 50 KB and 100 KB.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
