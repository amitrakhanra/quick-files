Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: QuickFile has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your files are processed inside your browser and are not uploaded by QuickFile. We do not set tracking or advertising cookies.

1. Who we are

QuickFile (quick-files.vercel.app) provides free PDF and image tools. For any privacy question, contact us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com.

2. Your files

When you use a tool, your file is opened and processed locally by code running in your browser. QuickFile does not send your files to its own servers and cannot see or store them. Once you close or refresh the page, the working data is gone. Because files stay on your device, please avoid processing highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted computers.

3. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, emails or documents through the tools. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

4. Third-party services

  • Hosting (Vercel): the site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type and requested pages to run and secure the service.
  • Processing libraries (cdnjs / Cloudflare): when a tool needs it, your browser downloads open-source libraries (such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, JSZip and jsPDF) from cdnjs. That request is made directly by your browser, so the CDN can see your IP address and browser details. Your files are not part of these requests.
  • Search (Google): we use Google Search Console to see how our pages appear in Google Search. It does not track you as a visitor of this site.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

QuickFile does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The site stores one small value in your browser's local storage to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice. You can remove it any time by clearing your browser's site data. Third-party hosts (see section 4) may set their own technical cookies or logs under their own policies; we do not control these.

If we add analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

6. Advertising

This site currently shows no ads.

7. Children

QuickFile is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data about visitors, there is usually nothing to access or delete. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data. If you have a request or question, email us and we will respond.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

10. Contact
