FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About PDF to JPG

Save the pages of a PDF as ordinary JPG pictures. This is useful when you need one page for a slide, a chat message or a form that only accepts images.

You can convert the whole document or just the pages you list, such as 1-3,5. Quality comes in three levels: Small (72 dpi), Standard (144 dpi) and High (216 dpi). One page downloads as a single JPG, and several pages download together as a ZIP file.

How to convert PDF pages to JPG

  1. Choose your PDF or drop it onto the box.
  2. Pick a quality: Small, Standard or High.
  3. Optionally type the pages you want, for example 1-3,5. Leave it blank for every page.
  4. Click Convert to JPG. Your image or ZIP downloads.

Tips and limits

  • Higher quality means bigger images. Use Standard for screens and High if you plan to print.
  • The current browser limit is 250 pages at a time. For a longer PDF, enter a page range or split it first.
  • A password-protected PDF must be opened with Unlock PDF first.

Frequently asked questions

Does it convert every page?

By default yes. Type a range like 2-4 in the Pages box to convert only those pages.

Why did I get a ZIP file?

When more than one page is converted, QuickFile bundles the JPG files into a single ZIP so you only download once.

Can I get the text out of the PDF instead?

Yes. PDF to Text saves the text into a .txt file, and PDF to Word saves it into a .docx file.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
