FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About Lock PDF

Add a password so that only people who know it can open your PDF. This is useful for sending a document by email or keeping a personal file away from casual snooping.

Choose a password of at least four characters and type it twice. QuickFile builds a new, locked copy in your browser. The locked copy is made from page images, so its text is not selectable, and it uses the basic password encryption built into the PDF format.

How to password-protect a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF or drop it onto the box.
  2. Type a password and confirm it by typing it again.
  3. Click Lock PDF.
  4. Download the locked copy and open it with your password to check it works.

Tips and limits

  • Write your password down somewhere safe. QuickFile cannot recover it, and nobody can open the file without it.
  • This is basic PDF protection and is fine for everyday use. It is not designed for highly sensitive or legal documents.
  • Keep an unlocked original in case you ever forget the password.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to set the password?

No. The password is added by code running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload your file.

Can I copy the text from a locked PDF?

No, because the locked copy is made from page images. If you need selectable text, keep the original as well.

How do I remove the password later?

Use Unlock PDF. You must know the password to do it.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
