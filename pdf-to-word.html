FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by QuickFile. Processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs when needed. Avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Are the tools free?

Yes, free with no sign-up.

Why do Strong compress, Unlock and Lock PDF turn text into images?

Those modes redraw each page as an image in your browser, which gives smaller files and removes restrictions, but text is no longer selectable.

How good is PDF to Word?

It extracts text only. Tables, columns and images are not reproduced, and scanned PDFs without real text are not supported.

Found a bug or want a new tool?

Email us at QuickfilesV1.1@gmail.com. If a tool shows an error, include the message and the tool name.

Processing limits

To protect browser stability, this version limits individual files to 100 MB, combined selections to 200 MB, PDF rendering to 250 pages at a time, and resized images to 40 megapixels. Some older browsers or low-memory devices may have lower practical limits.

About PDF to Word

Get the text of a PDF into an editable Word (.docx) document, so you can copy, change and reuse it. QuickFile reads the text from each page and writes it into a new Word file, with a page break between pages.

This is a text-only conversion. Layout, columns, tables, pictures and fonts are not reproduced, so expect to tidy the result. Scanned PDFs that contain only pictures of pages have no real text to read, and OCR is not supported.

How to convert a PDF to Word

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Click Convert to Word.
  3. Wait while each page is read.
  4. Download your .docx file and open it in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Tips and limits

  • This works best for plain text documents such as essays and notes.
  • If you get a message that no selectable text was found, your PDF is a scan and this tool cannot read it.
  • For plain text without Word, use PDF to Text. The current limit is 250 pages.

Frequently asked questions

Will the layout stay the same?

No. Only the text is extracted, not the layout, tables or images. Think of it as a way to get editable text, not a perfect copy.

Does it work on scanned documents?

No. A scan is a picture of text, and QuickFile does not do OCR, so it will tell you no selectable text was found.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The text is read and the .docx is built inside your browser.

Is it private?

Your file is opened and processed by JavaScript running in your browser, and QuickFile does not upload it. The processing libraries are downloaded from cdnjs the first time a tool needs them. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
